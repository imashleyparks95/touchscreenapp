- Active 3D field controls: **left stick** moves your bounded camera-relative ground cursor; **LT** gives precision. **D-pad** cycles available troops. **A** places a selected troop, or picks/confirms an existing move. **X** picks your own placed troop. **B** cancels a held move; with nothing held, it returns both pads to menu cursors. Use **GAMEPAD: FIELD** to return. P1 has a cyan circle, P2 an amber diamond.
