# Creative Freedom — two original games

The existing 25 public games cover reflex tapping, paint/music/media creation, circuit puzzles, platform/sling physics, tactical armies, tower defense, repairs/care, education and third-person fantasy combat. These additions introduce aerial courier navigation and indirect flock/current steering. No existing game is replaced. Base: v6.32.0 / b20a3dc; authoritative runtime dist/.

**Cloudwake Couriers (3D):** sculpted sail-wing aircraft deliver parcels among four floating harbor towns. Momentum, altitude, braking, moving storm fronts and optional elevated wind rings make route planning matter. Six increasingly demanding routes, five difficulties, timed solo or simultaneous cooperative flights, per-courier parcel ownership and shared team completion. Left stick/WASD/touch stick steers; right stick Y/Q/E/up-down buttons changes altitude; A/F docks, X/Space boosts, B brakes. Independent P2 controls/two native controllers. Babylon/Havok scene lifecycle, physics helpers, effects/audio and GamepadHub are reused unchanged. Game-owned original lofted aircraft/island meshes, harbor architecture, animated propellers/rigging, water/sky layers, wind and weather; quality settings, readable dock beacons and bounded scene budgets.

**Inkfin Lagoon (2D):** illustrated cut-paper fish move as a school through six authored living reefs. Hold a water lure to attract nearby fish; drag the lure to guide them, or use the repel tool to push them away from hazards. Two players own independent lures, cooperating to return the school to its illuminated sanctuary before the tide ends. Currents, rocks, moving jellyfish, stamina and restorative vents create indirect steering decisions rather than direct path following. Five difficulties, rescue thresholds, lost-fish recovery and replayable score challenges. Touch/mouse first, optional two controller lures and keyboard controls. Original layered Canvas seascape, fish/kelp/coral illustrations, fins, caustics, bubbles and distinct original soft-water synth cues.
