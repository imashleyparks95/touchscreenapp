# Milestone 7B — original care-game artwork

`dist/js/care-art.js` authors local Canvas kitchen/clinic tile walls, shelves/jars/pendant lights and shaded enamel crown/root paths. Existing patient silhouettes, monster bodies, ingredient illustrations, tool drawings and fonts are reused with their existing provenance. No stock media, external service, copied character, generated binary model or paid dependency was added. `touch-render.js` preserves original rule coordinates and overlays matching-tool outlines; `refine-render.js` adds named patience-sensitive guests in the original Café.

Original asset/vendor/license files, hosting identity and immutable migration checksums are unchanged. This does not relicense the whole app or its bundled third-party components. Browser screenshots use synthetic profiles, actual scenes and documented input/timeout fixtures; physical device verification remains separate.
