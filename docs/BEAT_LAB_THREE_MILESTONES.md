## BL2 — Piano Roll, instruments and collaborative performance

Combines Phase B and instrument/performance parts of D. Place/move/resize/delete/duplicate notes; chords/polyphony, long notes, velocities, grouped select/copy/paste, undo/redo. Whole through 32nd divisions where practical. Patterns store note timing/duration/velocity/instrument and join the same Playlist/clock/mixer/export/save path as drums.

Provide audibly distinct piano/electric piano/organ; electric/synth/sub/808 bass; lead/pad/pluck/retro synth; guitar-style/strings/bells/flute-style instruments plus existing drums. Use original Web Audio synthesis or legally redistributable assets with provenance. Optional ADSR/filter/volume/pan and octave controls, playable preview keyboard. Do not label identical oscillators as distinct instruments or claim sampled realism for synthesis.

Record actual keyboard/instrument/pad performances with clock-correct timing. Support independent multitouch drum-plus-keyboard and practical two-player instrument use; controller tab/instrument/transport/pad navigation, without forcing complex note editing onto pads. Pause/background/project/exit release voices and all owned resources. Save notes/instruments and restore old songs, shared sound/library/battle without changes. All Playlist parts must really render/export together.

Acceptance: original drum sequence → bass four bars → piano chords/melody on separate tracks; sustained/overlapping notes; player contribution isolation/shared recording; BPM/seek/loop and real mixer effects; save/close/reopen; inspect live/offline rendered output to prove all note/drum tracks and timing are present.
