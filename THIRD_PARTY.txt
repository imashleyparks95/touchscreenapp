Little Big Arcade — third-party audio encoder

LAME / lamejs 1.2.1, by Alex Zhukov and LAME contributors.
Upstream: https://github.com/zhuker/lamejs
LAME: https://lame.sourceforge.net

The browser encoder is distributed as a separate, unmodified lame.min.js.
This encoder converts PCM audio into genuine MPEG Layer III (MP3) files.
